Company-Wide Manager Tool
⚠️ Hard rule: A manager can offer up to $500 on their own authority (must provide a write-up for the decision). Any discount above that must be reviewed with your Branch Manager and Regional Manager (RM) or the CET (Customer Experience Team Lead) before it goes to the customer. It's the dollar amount that matters, not the percentage — a $300 discount on a $600 move is fine; a $700 discount on a $4,000 move needs a sign-off. This tool flags it when you cross the line.
